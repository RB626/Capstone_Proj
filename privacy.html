1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal and account information that you voluntarily provide when creating an account, signing in, or using our services. This may include your name, email address, profile photo, job preferences, and account authentication details.

  • Account details such as full name, email, and profile image.
  • Login information from authentication providers such as Facebook or Google.
  • Profile data you choose to add, such as work experience, skills, and employment details.
  • Basic device and usage information needed to improve platform performance and security.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate the platform, verify users, personalize the experience, improve our services, and maintain account security. We may also use your information to communicate updates related to your account or the platform.

We only use personal data for legitimate platform purposes, such as account access, job matching, profile management, user support, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

3. Facebook Login Data

If you sign in using Facebook Login, we may receive limited profile information from Facebook, depending on the permissions you approve. This may include your public profile, name, email address, and profile picture.

We use this information only to create or authenticate your BlueCollar Connect account and to provide a smoother sign-in experience. We do not sell Facebook profile data or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only when needed to operate our service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights, security, and integrity of our users and platform.

  • With service providers that help us host or support the platform.
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • To investigate fraud, abuse, or threats to platform safety.

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to help protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. No system is completely secure, but we continuously work to maintain a safe environment for our users.

6. Data Retention

We keep user information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce platform policies. When information is no longer needed, we will delete or anonymize it when reasonably possible.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You may request updates, corrections, or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable law and legitimate business requirements. You may also stop using the platform at any time.

  • Request access to data associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate profile information.
  • Request deletion of your account and connected data.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at rbbelas54@gmail.com.

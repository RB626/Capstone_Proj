User Data Deletion

Request deletion of your account and data.

This page explains how BlueCollar Connect users can request deletion of their account information, profile data, and connected platform records, including data created through Facebook Login or other sign-in methods.

Support email rbbelas54@gmail.com
Response target Within 7 business days

How to request deletion

If you would like your BlueCollar Connect account and associated personal data removed, send a deletion request using the support email below. Please include enough information for us to identify your account and process your request securely.

1

Email your request

Send an email to rbbelas54@gmail.com with the subject line: Account Deletion Request.

2

Include your account details

Include your full name, registered email address, and any other information that can help us identify your account, such as your phone number or linked login provider.

3

Wait for verification

We may contact you to verify that the request came from the real account owner before deleting any data.

4

Deletion processing

Once verified, we will process the deletion request and remove or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, unless retention is required for legal or security reasons.

What may be deleted

  • Account profile information such as name, email, and photo.
  • Saved work experience, profile details, and related account settings.
  • Authentication-related records connected to your BlueCollar Connect profile.
  • Other personal data stored for normal operation of the platform, subject to technical and legal limitations.

What may be retained

Some limited information may be retained when required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security investigations, dispute resolution, or enforcement of platform policies.

Need to include these details

To help us process your deletion request faster, include your registered email, full name, and whether your account was created using Facebook, Google, phone number, or email/password.

Important

Submitting a deletion request may permanently remove access to your account, profile information, saved work experience, and other related data stored in BlueCollar Connect.
